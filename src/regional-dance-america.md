        <h3>REGIONAL DANCE AMERICA</h3>
        <p> RDA is a prestigious national organization that celebrates excellence in pre-professional dance. As part of the Lake Erie Ballet Company’s RDA Ensemble, dancers will train, perform, and connect with other talented artists from across the country. Open to dancers from all studios, regardless of where they currently train, this program is designed to make elite training and performance opportunities more accessible—helping aspiring artists take the next step in their dance journey.  

By participating in Regional Dance America, dancers will engage with peers and professionals from across the country, building both their skills and their confidence on a national stage. This program reflects our mission to provide access and opportunity to all dancers—regardless of studio affiliation—ensuring that talent, passion, and potential are what open the door to transformative experiences in dance.
</p>
